# More With More: phase 2 design notes ("The Campus")

Phase 1 ends when leased capacity runs out ("Break ground on your own campus"). In phase 2 you
build and power your own site, and the company quietly turns from a lab into an infra company.

## Resources
- **Land** (acres), **Power** (MW), **Water** (gal/day of cooling)
- **Political capital**: economic-development promises, tax deals, donations
- **Community opposition**: rises with land, water and power use; slows permits
- **Backlog**: signed capacity contracts. Rounds and the debt facility are sized on backlog, not
  model generations (the infra-company framing: investors fund contracts)

## Systems
1. **Pick a county**: cheap land / weak grid; strong grid / drought; big incentives / organized town
2. **Power**: grid interconnection queue (a countdown you can skip with political capital); on-site
   gas turbines (fast, +opposition); solar + batteries (slow, land-hungry); restart a nuclear plant;
   buy the utility. Supply chain: transformer lead times; buy the transformer factory.
3. **Water**: capped municipal allocation (drought events); wells (aquifer meter only goes down);
   reclaimed water; desalination (needs more power); buy the lake
4. **Land and politics**: buy acres, rezone the suburbs, eminent domain (political capital, opposition spike)
5. **Money**: Parallax deals scale up; sovereign wealth; "Announce a $500B project" (announced vs
   built capacity counters; hype runs on announced)

## Private cloud deals (user idea)
Counterpart to the spot market: spot = short, market price, rewards timing; private deal = long,
locked, lower rate, **paid up front**.
- Offers arrive over time ("A lab funded by Parallax wants 20,000 GPUs for 15 min, starting in 6 min:
  $80M up front")
- Accepting pays now and reserves GPUs for the term (they don't serve or train)
- **You can sell capacity you haven't built yet**: the upfront cash funds the build that must deliver it
- Missing the start date: penalties, hype loss, lenders review the facility
- Signed deals add to backlog -> bigger rounds and debt draws

## Bubble (proposed for phase 1 too)
- Hype above 100 is froth: still counts for raising/borrowing, but accumulates risk
- Reality-check events ("Analyst note: where's the revenue?", "Someone read the S-1") remove a share
  of froth; the higher it's pumped, the harder the fall
- Real revenue relative to spend softens the crash (fundamentals only matter in the crash)
- A crash while deep in debt pauses draws

## Mini-games
- Town hall meetings (timed choices: political capital vs opposition; "10,000 jobs" / jobs delivered: 41)
- Interconnection queue race
- Spot market carries over

## Model lines
"The river is underutilized." / "Have you considered the aquifer?" / "I have reviewed the zoning code.
It is a suggestion."

## Ending
A gigawatt campus; the model asks for the next one (phase 3: planetary).
